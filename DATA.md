# Data requirements

The research data and generated products are maintained outside this GitHub
repository.

## Published reconstruction

The final extended record is available from the University of Arizona Research
Data Repository:

- **Global Snow and Ice Cover (1980–1987): An Extended GMASI Dataset**
- Version 1
- DOI: [10.25422/AZU.DATA.28012532.V1](https://doi.org/10.25422/AZU.DATA.28012532.V1)
- Coverage: 1 January 1980–30 June 1987
- Resolution: daily, 0.1° latitude–longitude grid

## Principal input datasets

- Global Multisensor Automated Snow and Ice Mapping System (GMASI)
- ERA5 reanalysis surface and near-surface variables
- Rutgers Northern Hemisphere Weekly Snow Cover Extent
- land–sea, lake-cover, elevation, and related static predictors used by the
  archived workflows

Consult the associated article for the authoritative scientific description,
training and validation periods, model variants, and consistency checks:
[https://doi.org/10.1029/2024EA004171](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024EA004171).

The paths embedded in the scripts document the original rain-server analysis
environment. They do not imply redistribution rights for third-party inputs.
